# Featured Image visual frame 0.2

Status: HUMAN-SELECTED EXPERIMENT · 2026-10-16

This frame freezes the visual language established by the Markdown, JavaScript
calculator and API/HTTP/JSON illustrations. It governs the next generated set and
prevents accidental palette or rendering drift. It is not an automatic media
generator or a requirement to recolor historical images.

## Identity

- Soft editorial comic with a slightly storybook-like world.
- Strong, slightly hand-drawn charcoal/near-black contours.
- Flat color fields with gentle paper texture, shallow shadows and limited soft
  modelling; never photorealistic or glossy 3D.
- One small active character, normally 20–27% of the canvas height.
- A large conceptual object, machine or environment carries 55–70% of the visual
  story.
- One readable action or journey, understood without labels.
- Enough secondary detail to reward a longer look while preserving a strong
  silhouette at article-card size.

## Closed palette

Only the following color families are allowed:

1. warm ivory / off-white — background and open space;
2. charcoal / near-black — outlines, hair, trousers and strong UI fields;
3. burnt orange / rust / restrained coral — primary accent and action;
4. pale beige / sand / warm gray — structures and supporting surfaces;
5. muted sky blue — data, flow, glass or one secondary object;
6. soft mustard yellow — signal lights and small highlights;
7. muted olive green — plants and tiny environmental accents only.

Do not introduce purple, saturated pink, neon cyan, vivid primary red or another
new accent family. Blue, yellow and green remain subordinate to rust orange and
charcoal. White flowers may use a small orange or yellow center.

## Composition and detail

- Opaque 16:9 landscape; final project asset 1600×900 WebP.
- Keep the important action inside a thumbnail-safe central area.
- Prefer a clear left-to-right journey or a central machine with visible stages.
- Use one person unless the concept genuinely requires otherwise.
- Small plants, flowers, clouds, rocks, cables and indicator lights may create the
  comic world, but cannot obscure the main technical metaphor.
- A single exception, rejected item or alternate branch may add narrative clarity.
- No prose, code, URLs, brand marks, trademarks or watermarks.

## Reference anchors

- `src/assets/markdown-podstawy.webp` — transformation and page-building balance;
- `src/assets/kalkulator-w-javascript.webp` — comic machine and staged action;
- `src/assets/api-http-json.webp` — detailed story world and controlled journey.

References define palette, line, texture, character scale and density. New scenes
must remain compositionally original and must not copy their objects or layouts.
